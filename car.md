# Cars

A car is a four-wheeled motor vehicle designed primarily for passenger transportation on roads. Modern cars are typically powered by internal combustion engines (burning petrol or diesel) or electric motors, and are equipped with systems for steering, braking, suspension, and climate control.

## Key Components

- **Engine / Motor**: Generates the power needed to move the vehicle.
- **Transmission**: Transfers power from the engine to the wheels.
- **Chassis & Body**: The structural frame and outer shell.
- **Suspension**: Absorbs shocks from the road for a smoother ride.
- **Braking System**: Slows and stops the vehicle safely.
- **Electrical System**: Powers lights, sensors, infotainment, and (in EVs) the drivetrain.

## Types of Cars

- **Sedan**: Four-door passenger car with a separate trunk.
- **Hatchback**: Rear door that swings upward, combining passenger and cargo space.
- **SUV**: Sport Utility Vehicle with higher ground clearance and often four-wheel drive.
- **Coupe**: Two-door car, usually with a sporty design.
- **Convertible**: Has a retractable roof for open-air driving.
- **Electric Vehicle (EV)**: Powered entirely by batteries and electric motors.

## Brief History

1. **1886** — Karl Benz patents the Benz Patent-Motorwagen, widely regarded as the first modern automobile.
2. **1908** — Ford introduces the Model T, making cars affordable via assembly-line production.
3. **1970s** — Oil crises drive demand for fuel-efficient and compact cars.
4. **2000s** — Hybrid vehicles like the Toyota Prius gain popularity.
5. **2020s** — Electric vehicles and autonomous driving features become mainstream.
