# The Ocean

The ocean covers about 71% of Earth's surface and is the largest habitat on the planet. It regulates climate, produces oxygen, and supports a vast diversity of life.

## The Five Oceans

1. **Pacific Ocean** — The largest and deepest ocean.
2. **Atlantic Ocean** — The second-largest, separating the Americas from Europe and Africa.
3. **Indian Ocean** — The third-largest, bordered by Asia, Africa, and Australia.
4. **Southern Ocean** — Surrounds Antarctica.
5. **Arctic Ocean** — The smallest and shallowest, located around the North Pole.

## Why Oceans Matter

- **Climate Regulation**: Ocean currents distribute heat around the globe.
- **Oxygen Production**: Marine plants and phytoplankton produce over half of the world's oxygen.
- **Food**: Billions of people rely on the ocean for seafood.
- **Transportation**: Most global trade travels by sea.

## Threats

- Plastic pollution
- Overfishing
- Ocean acidification
- Rising temperatures due to climate change
